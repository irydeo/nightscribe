NightScribe includes a copy of Find_Orb
=======================================

NightScribe bundles a copy of Find_Orb (Project Pluto), the orbit fitter it
uses to check your measurements against the published observations.

  * Find_Orb is Copyright (C) Bill Gray / Project Pluto.
  * It is distributed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 (or
    later), with an extra clause: use in or with commercial software is
    forbidden without written permission from Project Pluto.
  * NightScribe is a non-commercial, GPLv3 project, so it ships Find_Orb as
    a separate program (mere aggregation).
  * Source code:  https://github.com/Bill-Gray/find_orb
  * Project page: https://www.projectpluto.com/find_orb.htm

The optional JPL DE430t ephemerides (public domain) are NOT included: you
can download them from Settings (Measurement -> Find_Orb).
